## 4. Social publishing SOP

### Preflight
For every post:
- confirm exact company/account;
- confirm source asset ID;
- confirm image/product/talent is approved and current;
- verify dates, venue, lineup and CTA;
- apply trackable URL/code where appropriate;
- spellcheck caption and on-image copy;
- review mobile crop;
- record publish approval;
- publish/schedule only to the matching brand account;
- preserve provider post ID/permalink and published timestamp.

### Nightmare daily baseline
- 2 primary feed posts/day
- 6–10 stories/day
- 1–2 Reels/TikToks/day
- final week: increase only with performance and audience-frequency controls.

### Engagement
- first response sweep within 15 minutes of priority post;
- second sweep at +60 minutes;
- then every 2–3 hours during active campaign window;
- route ticket questions to Ticket Face/Ticketmaster;
- route VIP to concierge lane;
- route sponsor inquiries to sponsorship pipeline;
- route promoter/ambassador interest to onboarding;
- never promise artist access, backstage access, discounts or inventory unless verified.

Record: comment/DM category, response status, conversion intent, link/code used, GHL/CRM routing result and opt-out/safety flags.

## 6. CRM / email / SMS SOP

Required segments:
- buyer
- nonbuyer
- ticket-face visitor
- Ticketmaster clickout without verified purchase
- promo-code visitor
- high-intent repeat visitor
- VIP/couch/booth lead
- promoter-referred
- creator-referred
- merch buyer without ticket
- ticket buyer without merch
- Tampa / Orlando / Atlanta / feeder market
- suppressed / unsubscribed / invalid

Behavior recovery:
- +15 min contextual reminder where consent and channel allow
- +3h email or contextual follow-up
- +24h segmented email/SMS
- +48h urgency, alternate offer or concierge route

Never send a generic daily blast to the full file. Respect consent, suppression, frequency caps, buyer status and deliverability.

## 7. Outreach SOP

For sponsor, creator, promoter, podcast, DJ, host, model and venue outreach:
1. verify fit and contact route;
2. personalize the reason for fit;
3. attach/use the correct deck or role packet;
4. state the exact ask and CTA;
5. log first-touch timestamp and provider receipt;
6. schedule next step only if permitted;
7. stop on decline/opt-out;
8. move the opportunity stage based on a real response, not intent;
9. retain meeting/proposal/contract/payment evidence.

Sponsor stages:
Identified → Verified → First Touch → Follow-Up → Interested → Meeting → Proposal → Negotiation → Verbal Yes → Contract → Invoice → Paid → Activation → Fulfillment Report.
